Restaurant A – Meghana's Biryani

The chicken biryani was extremely delicious and full of flavour.
Meghana's special biryani is worth the price.
The quantity was good and sufficient for one person.
The biryani was spicy but perfectly cooked.
The service was good and staff were friendly.
The restaurant was crowded during lunch hours.
The waiting time was too long on weekends.
The food quality was excellent and consistent.
The packaging was neat and suitable for takeaway.
Overall, a great place for biryani lovers.


Restaurant B – Mallika Biryani

The biryani tasted good but lacked enough spices.
The chicken pieces were fresh and properly cooked.
The prices were reasonable compared to other restaurants.
The portion size was slightly disappointing.
The food was served hot and fresh.
The staff were polite but service was sometimes slow.
The restaurant ambience was simple and comfortable.
The takeaway packaging was good without any leakage.
The taste was good but could be improved.
Overall, a decent restaurant for enjoying biryani with family.
